FAQ

About the Decision Matrix

Is this only for technology courses?

No. The matrix includes fields such as business, commerce, science, arts, design, engineering, health, law and education.

Can I compare any two courses?

The system is designed so that courses can be added and compared across different fields as the platform grows.

Does the matrix tell me which course is best?

No. Different courses suit different goals and circumstances. The matrix shows differences and trade-offs so you can make your own decision.

Can my priorities change the comparison?

Yes. Your priorities are important because the same course can make sense for one student and not for another depending on goals, budget, time and interests.

Does the result guarantee a career or job?

No. The matrix is a decision-support tool and does not guarantee employment, salary or career outcomes.
