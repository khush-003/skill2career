NEW TO CAREER PLANNING?

Let's clear up the basics.

You don't need to understand everything before using Skill2Career. Start with the questions you already have.

What is Skill2Career?
Skill2Career is a career exploration and learning guidance platform. It brings careers, courses, skills, roadmaps and learning resources together so students can understand their possible paths more clearly.
What problem is Skill2Career trying to solve?
Career information is often scattered across different websites and resources. A student may know the name of a course but not know what careers it can lead to, which skills are useful, or what they should learn next. Skill2Career connects these pieces.
Who can use Skill2Career?
Students and learners exploring education, careers, skills or learning paths can use the platform to organize their exploration and understand possible next steps.
What is a career?
A career represents the type of professional work a person may pursue. A career page can explain what the role involves, typical activities, skills, education paths and related opportunities.
What is a course?
A course is a structured learning or educational path. It can be a degree, diploma, certificate, professional program or another form of education. Different courses can connect to different careers.
What is a skill?
A skill is an ability that you develop through learning and practice. Different careers require different combinations of technical, analytical, creative, communication or other skills.
Why do I need a roadmap?
A roadmap helps organize learning. Instead of asking "What should I learn next?" every time, you can see how foundational knowledge, skills, practice and projects can fit together.
Is a roadmap the same as a course?
No. A course is something you study. A roadmap is a structured path that can connect multiple courses, skills, resources and projects toward a particular goal.
Do I need a degree for every career?
Different careers have different education and qualification requirements. Some may require specific qualifications, while other roles can place significant emphasis on skills, experience, projects or certifications. The requirements should be checked for the specific career.
What if I don't know which career to choose?
You can start by exploring different career areas, understanding what the work involves, looking at required skills and checking possible education paths. You do not need to know your final career before beginning the exploration process.
Can I change my roadmap later?
Yes. Your learning path can change as your interests, skills and goals change. Exploring another career can also reveal skills that overlap with what you have already learned.
Why are projects part of the roadmap?
Projects allow you to apply concepts instead of only reading or watching lessons. They can help you identify gaps in your understanding and can eventually demonstrate your practical skills.
What are resources?
Resources are learning materials connected to a career, course, skill or roadmap stage. They can include tutorials, documentation, books, videos, practice platforms and other references. The platform can also display the source of each resource.
What is Skill Gap Analysis?
Skill Gap Analysis helps identify the difference between skills associated with a target career and the skills a learner currently has. This can help identify areas that may need more learning or practice.
What is the Decision Matrix?
The Decision Matrix is a structured decision-support feature. It can help organize factors such as career goals, current skills, time, financial priorities, specialization needs and alternative paths when comparing education or career options.
Where should I start if I am completely confused?
Start with Careers. Understand what different careers involve, then explore the skills associated with the careers that interest you. After that, look at courses and roadmaps to understand possible learning paths.
Does following a roadmap guarantee a job?
No. A roadmap is a planning and learning tool. Career outcomes depend on many factors including skills, qualifications, experience, projects, hiring requirements and individual circumstances.
Is Skill2Career itself a learning platform?
Its primary purpose is to organize career and learning guidance. It can connect users to useful resources while helping them understand careers, courses, skills and learning paths. It does not need to replace dedicated learning platforms.
How should I use Skill2Career?
A simple journey is: explore a career → understand its requirements → explore courses → identify skills → follow a roadmap → use resources → build projects → review skill gaps → prepare for your next step.
