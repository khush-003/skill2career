Frequently asked questions

A few things to know about the Skill2Career resource library.

What type of resources can I find here?

The library is designed for notes, study materials, subject references, career guides and learning roadmaps.

Can I find resources according to my course?

Yes. Resources can be organized around courses, subjects, academic levels and learning goals.

How do resources connect with careers?

Resources can be linked with the subjects and skills required for particular career paths.

Are all resources official study materials?

Resource sources should be clearly identified so that learners can understand where each material comes from.
